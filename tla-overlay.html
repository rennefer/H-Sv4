TLA Fuzzy Search

A search panel that lays over the Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae and forgives typos in transliteration, translation and hieroglyphs.

Install (once, about a minute)

  1. Add the free Tampermonkey extension to your browser: Chrome · Edge · Firefox
  2. Click Install TLA fuzzy and press Install in the Tampermonkey tab that opens.

In Chrome or Edge, if the search bar doesn't appear: open the browser's Extensions page, click Details on Tampermonkey and switch on Allow User Scripts (Chrome may call it Developer mode).

Use

Open thesaurus-linguae-aegyptiae.de and pass its "Verify" check. A TLA fuzzy search bar is waiting in the top-right corner of every page: type and press Enter. Click a result to preview it in the panel; Ctrl+click opens it in a new tab; the – button folds the panel back into the bar.

No extensions allowed? Use a bookmark instead

What you can type

nefer, hr, djehuty, sthPlain letters. h/s/t/d/k also match ḥ ḫ ẖ / š z / ṯ / ḏ / q; a matches ꜣ ꜥ; e and o are treated as helper vowels.
nrf, nfrrTypos: one wrong, missing, extra or swapped letter still finds nfr.
beutiful, schoenEnglish or German meanings, with spelling fixed automatically.
F35 I9 D21, G5, or pasted signsGardiner codes or Unicode hieroglyphs; one sign off, missing or extra still matches.

Use the chips in the panel to limit a search to one field, and the checkboxes to include personal names or the Demotic lemma list.

Notes

Results come live from the TLA's own search, fetched with your browser's verified session, a few requests at a time. Data: Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae, Berlin-Brandenburgische Akademie der Wissenschaften, CC BY-SA 4.0. English spelling list from Hunspell en_US.
